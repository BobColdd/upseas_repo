Before you begin

  1. A short countdown runs after you press Start task, then the test opens by itself.
  2. Reading and Writing: the timer starts as soon as the test opens. Listening: the recording starts by itself and the test closes shortly after it ends.
  3. Timers cannot be paused. Make sure you are ready.
  4. Do not refresh or close the page. Answers are only saved when you submit.
  5. When time runs out, your answers are submitted for you.
  6. After you submit you cannot change your answers. A submitted task can only be retaken if your tutor reopens it.

What to expect

Listening about 30 min

4 sections, 40 questions. Use headphones and check your volume during the countdown. The recording cannot be paused or replayed. Write answers as you listen and check your spelling.

Reading 60 min

3 passages, 40 questions. Spend about 20 minutes on each passage. Answer every question: a wrong answer does not lose marks.

Writing 60 min

Task 1 (150 words) and Task 2 (250 words). Task 2 carries more marks, so spend about 40 minutes on it. Plan briefly and leave a minute to check your work.

Speaking 11–14 min

3 parts. Find a quiet place and answer out loud. In Part 2 you get about one minute to prepare. Speak naturally and extend your answers with reasons and examples.
